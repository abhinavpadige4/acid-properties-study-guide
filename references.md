# References

Authoritative sources for further reading on ACID properties.

## Official Documentation

- **PostgreSQL — Transaction Isolation**
  https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/transaction-iso.html
  The definitive reference for isolation levels and concurrency behavior in PostgreSQL.

- **MySQL 8.0 — InnoDB Transaction Model**
  https://dev.mysql.com/doc/refman/8.0/en/innodb-transaction-model.html
  Covers ACID guarantees, isolation levels, and locking in MySQL's InnoDB engine.

- **Oracle Database — Concepts: Transactions**
  https://docs.oracle.com/en/database/oracle/oracle-database/19/cncon/transactions.html
  Oracle's explanation of ACID and transaction management.

- **SQL Server — Transactions**
  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sql/t-sql/language-elements/transactions-transact-sql
  Microsoft's guide to T-SQL transactions and isolation levels.

## Academic & Foundational

- **Gray, J. N., et al. (1992). "Granularity of Data and Consistency in Concurrency Control."**
  The classic paper that formalized ACID and isolation levels.

- **Härder, T., & Reuter, A. (1983). "Properties of Database Recoverable Systems."**
  Early formalization of recovery and durability guarantees.

- **Brewer, P. A. (2000). "CAP: There's No Free Lunch."**
  The original CAP theorem paper. https://www.cs.berkeley.edu/~brewer/cs262b/cap.pdf

## Tutorials & Explanations

- **GeeksforGeeks — ACID Properties in DBMS**
  https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/acid-properties-in-dbms/
  A beginner-friendly walkthrough with examples.

- **Database Relational — ACID Properties**
  https://www.databaserelational.com/acid-properties/
  Clear definitions and real-world examples.

- **YouTube — ACID Properties Explained**
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5FkY0uYcG6I
  Visual explanation with animations.

## Further Reading

- **Martin Kleppmann, "Designing Data-Intensive Applications" (2017)**
  Chapters 5 (Transactions) and 7 (Consistency and Consensus) are the gold standard for understanding ACID in modern systems.

- **Jepsen.io**
  https://jepsen.io/
  Real-world testing of distributed databases against ACID and CAP guarantees.
